IP Browser opens websites from your own server. Websites see one address — your server's IP — wherever you are and whatever network you use.

Handy when a service lets in only one IP address, or when you want your accounts to always see the same address.

A REAL BROWSER
A start page with favorites and recent sites, tabs with page previews, bookmarks and history (kept on your phone only), find on page and sharing. Your server and data controls live in Settings.

YOUR SERVER IN A FEW TAPS
Rent any small VPS with Ubuntu or Debian, enter its address and password, tap Install. IP Browser sets everything up and forgets the password. Or add a server someone gave you, as a key or a QR code.

ONE ADDRESS, ALWAYS
• Pages, their address lookups and downloads load only from your server.
• If the server cannot be reached, the browser waits: it never switches to your own connection.
• Before the first page, IP Browser checks which address websites see.
• Websites see your server's time zone.
• Works on Wi-Fi or mobile data; the rest of your phone keeps its own connection.

CHECK IT YOURSELF
Settings → Leak test shows the address and time zone websites see.

NO ACCOUNTS, NO ANALYTICS
No sign-up, no tracking, no ads, and no servers of ours: the app talks only to your server and the websites you open.

Open source: github.com/viktorvich/ip-browser
